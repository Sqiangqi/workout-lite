# Workout Lite

A phone web app for logging gym sessions into your own Google Sheet: today's plan, sets and reps, progress charts and an exercise library.

- Open the page and sign in with Google. On first open, one tap makes a **Workout Lite** folder in your My Drive with two Sheets: *Workout Lite log* (exercises, sessions, week plans) and *Workout Lite food* (food log, quick foods, body weight). You can also pick a Sheet you already have.
- Your data stays in your Google account: the two Sheets, plus small settings files (and unsaved sets) in the same Workout Lite folder. The app can only see files it made itself, unless you choose to use a Sheet you already have. Nothing is stored on this site.
- On iPhone: open it in Safari, then Share > Add to Home Screen.
