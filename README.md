# Workout Lite

A phone web app for logging gym sessions into your own Google Sheet: today's plan, sets and reps, progress charts and an exercise library.

- Open the page, sign in with Google, then create your own workout Sheet or pick an existing one.
- Your data stays in your Google account (your Sheet, plus a hidden app folder in your Drive for unsaved sets). Nothing is stored on this site.
- On iPhone: open it in Safari, then Share > Add to Home Screen.
