Workout Lite: privacy

Last updated November 7, 2026

In short

Workout Lite keeps your workouts and food in your own Google Drive. The app has no server and no database of its own: everything runs in your browser, and nothing about you is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What the app can see in your Google account

  • Your email address, to show who is signed in and to keep each person's setup separate.
  • Only the files the app made itself: the Workout Lite folder in your My Drive, its two Google Sheets (Workout Lite log and Workout Lite food) and the small settings files in that folder. It cannot see your other files.
  • Only if you choose "Use a Sheet I already have": Google asks you for permission to open your Google Sheets, so the app can read and write the Sheet you pick.

What the app stores and where

  • Your sessions, exercises, week plans, food log and body weight are saved in those Sheets, in your Google Drive.
  • Your browser keeps a copy and your sign-in for this site on your device, so the app opens quickly. Signing out removes them.
  • Nothing is stored on this website or sold, shared or used for advertising.

Google user data

Workout Lite's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Delete the Workout Lite folder in your Google Drive, and remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
